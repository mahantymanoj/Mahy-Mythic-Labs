# AI Generation Rules — Mahy Mythic Labs

## 1. Purpose

Defines standards for using artificial intelligence in content creation.

The goal is to use AI as a creative accelerator while maintaining quality, originality, and authenticity.

---

# 2. AI Philosophy

AI is:

```text
A Creative Partner

Not

A Replacement For Storytelling
```

Human direction remains responsible for:

* Ideas
* Quality
* Final decisions

---

# 3. AI Generated Content Standards

All AI-generated assets must be:

* High quality
* Consistent
* Purpose-driven
* Reviewed before use

---

# 4. Image Generation Rules

Images should maintain:

## Style

* Cinematic realism
* Documentary quality
* High detail

## Composition

Include:

* Clear subject
* Strong lighting
* Depth
* Storytelling elements

---

# 5. Video Generation Rules

Generated videos should include:

* Natural motion
* Cinematic camera movement
* Realistic physics
* Environmental details

Avoid:

* Random movement
* Distorted characters
* Unrealistic animation

---

# 6. Character Consistency Rules

Recurring characters require:

* Fixed appearance
* Fixed clothing
* Fixed attributes
* Reference images

Maintain character bible.

---

# 7. Prompt Engineering Rules

Every prompt should define:

```text
Subject

Environment

Camera

Lighting

Mood

Style

Negative Prompt
```

---

# 8. AI Quality Control

Every AI output must be checked for:

## Visual Errors

* Extra limbs
* Distorted faces
* Incorrect details

## Story Errors

* Wrong context
* Wrong symbolism

## Technical Errors

* Resolution
* Aspect ratio
* Artifacts

---

# 9. AI Model Tracking

Record:

* Model used
* Prompt
* Parameters
* Date
* Version

---

# 10. Ethical AI Rules

Never create:

* Fake news
* Misleading historical claims
* Harmful content
* Unauthorized impersonations

---

# 11. AI Workflow Rule

Standard process:

```text
Idea

↓

Prompt Design

↓

Generation

↓

Review

↓

Improvement

↓

Final Asset
```

---

# 12. Future AI Agent Rule

AI agents must follow:

```text
Production Bible

+

Brand Identity

+

Quality Standards
```

before creating any content.
