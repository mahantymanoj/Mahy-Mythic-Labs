# Preface

Creating a video is the beginning of the journey.

Publishing it successfully requires another discipline.

YouTube is not only a video platform.

It is a recommendation system powered by:

* Viewer interest.
* Satisfaction.
* Retention.
* Engagement.
* Consistency.

A great video that nobody discovers has limited impact.

A well-packaged video with meaningful content can reach millions.

Mahy Mythic Labs approaches YouTube as a long-term storytelling platform.

# Chapter 6 — The First 30 Seconds Rule

The opening decides retention.

The first 30 seconds should contain:

* Strong visual.
* Interesting question.
* Clear promise.

Avoid:

* Long greetings.
* Channel introduction.
* Slow explanation.

# Chapter 9 — Thumbnail + Title Relationship

Thumbnail and title should work together.

Bad:

Thumbnail:
"Ancient Temple"

Title:
"History of Ancient Architecture"

No curiosity.

---

Good:

Thumbnail:
"Hidden Door"

Title:
"The Ancient Civilization That Vanished Without A Trace"

Creates a question.
