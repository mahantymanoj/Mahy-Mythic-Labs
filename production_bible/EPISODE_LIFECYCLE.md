# Preface

Every YouTube episode is treated as a complete creative project.

An episode is not just a video file.

It is a combination of:

* Research.
* Story.
* Visual design.
* Audio.
* Editing.
* Publishing.
* Learning.

Mahy Mythic Labs follows a repeatable lifecycle to transform ideas into cinematic experiences.

The objective:

> Build a production system where every episode becomes better than the previous one.
