# © Copyright Policy

> **Mahy Mythic Labs Copyright & Intellectual Property Policy**
>
> Version: 1.0
>
> This document defines the copyright, licensing, attribution, and intellectual property standards for all content produced by Mahy Mythic Labs. It establishes mandatory rules to ensure that every published asset is legally compliant, ethically sourced, and safe for commercial distribution.

---

# 1. Purpose

This policy protects:

- Mahy Mythic Labs
- Content creators
- AI-generated productions
- Viewers
- Commercial monetization

Every asset used within the platform must have a clear legal basis for use.

---

# 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- Research
- Scripts
- Images
- Videos
- Narration
- Music
- Sound Effects
- Fonts
- Logos
- Animations
- Thumbnails
- Metadata
- AI-generated assets
- Source references

Every production workflow must comply with this policy.

---

# 3. Core Principles

Mahy Mythic Labs follows five principles:

✓ Originality

✓ Attribution where required

✓ Respect for creators

✓ Commercial safety

✓ Legal compliance

If there is uncertainty regarding licensing, the asset should not be used until verified.

---

# 4. Ownership

Unless otherwise noted:

- Original scripts belong to Mahy Mythic Labs.
- Original prompts belong to Mahy Mythic Labs.
- Original workflow designs belong to Mahy Mythic Labs.
- Original automation code belongs to Mahy Mythic Labs.
- Original branding belongs to Mahy Mythic Labs.

Ownership of AI-generated outputs depends on the applicable provider terms and local laws. Review provider licenses before commercial use.

---

# 5. Acceptable Sources

Preferred sources include:

- Public domain works
- Government publications
- Creative Commons resources (when license permits)
- Self-created assets
- Commercially licensed assets
- AI-generated assets with commercial usage rights

Maintain documentation for every externally sourced asset.

---

# 6. Prohibited Content

Do NOT use:

- Copyrighted images without permission
- Copyrighted videos without permission
- Copyrighted music without permission
- Pirated content
- Paid assets without a valid license
- Trademark misuse
- Unauthorized logos
- Watermarked content
- Downloaded content with unclear licensing

When in doubt, replace the asset.

---

# 7. Research Usage

Research may reference:

- Academic publications
- Books
- Museums
- Scientific organizations
- Government agencies
- Reputable educational resources

Facts may be summarized, but large sections of copyrighted text must not be reproduced.

---

# 8. Scripts

Scripts should:

- Be original.
- Use independent wording.
- Avoid copying existing documentaries.
- Avoid copying articles.
- Avoid copying books.

Research should inform the script, not replace it.

---

# 9. Images

Images must be:

- Original
- Properly licensed
- Public domain
- Commercially licensed
- AI-generated under a license permitting commercial use

Avoid using screenshots, artwork, or photographs with uncertain rights.

---

# 10. Video

Video assets should:

- Be original
- Be AI-generated
- Be self-recorded
- Be commercially licensed

Stock footage should only be used according to its license terms.

---

# 11. Music

Preferred sources:

- Original compositions
- Royalty-free libraries
- Licensed music
- Commercial subscriptions

Avoid copyrighted commercial music unless explicit rights have been obtained.

Maintain records of music licenses.

---

# 12. Sound Effects

Sound effects should originate from:

- Commercial libraries
- Royalty-free collections
- Original recordings

License information should be retained.

---

# 13. Fonts

Fonts used in branding or thumbnails must allow the intended commercial use.

Do not redistribute fonts unless the license permits it.

---

# 14. AI-Generated Content

AI-generated assets should comply with:

- Provider terms of service
- Commercial usage policies
- Attribution requirements (if applicable)

Avoid prompts that intentionally imitate a specific living artist's unique style or attempt to recreate copyrighted works.

---

# 15. Attribution

Provide attribution whenever required by the asset license.

Maintain a record of:

- Asset
- Source
- License
- Creator
- Date acquired

Store this information within the repository or accompanying documentation.

---

# 16. Source Tracking

Every externally sourced asset should include:

- Source URL
- License type
- Creator
- Download date
- Usage rights
- Notes

This information should be stored alongside the asset or in a centralized asset register.

---

# 17. Trademarks

Do not:

- Misrepresent affiliation
- Misuse logos
- Imply endorsement
- Modify protected trademarks in misleading ways

Use trademarks only when necessary for factual or educational discussion.

---

# 18. YouTube Compliance

Every uploaded video should comply with:

- YouTube Community Guidelines
- YouTube Copyright Policies
- YouTube Partner Program requirements

Content should be reviewed before publication to reduce the risk of copyright claims or monetization issues.

---

# 19. AI Agent Responsibilities

Each AI agent has defined responsibilities:

Research Agent

- Record sources.
- Identify factual references.

Script Agent

- Produce original wording.

Image Agent

- Use approved prompts and licensed references.

Video Agent

- Use approved visual assets.

Narration Agent

- Use licensed voices or approved text-to-speech services.

Publishing Agent

- Verify attribution requirements.
- Include required credits.

Quality Agent

- Confirm compliance before release.

---

# 20. Copyright Review Checklist

Before publishing, verify:

✓ Script is original

✓ Images are licensed or original

✓ Video assets are licensed or original

✓ Music is licensed

✓ Sound effects are licensed

✓ Fonts permit commercial use

✓ Required attribution is included

✓ Sources are documented

✓ Provider terms have been reviewed where applicable

---

# 21. Incident Response

If a potential copyright issue is identified:

1. Suspend publication.
2. Remove the affected asset.
3. Replace with a compliant alternative.
4. Update documentation.
5. Re-run quality review.
6. Record the resolution for future reference.

---

# 22. Continuous Review

Copyright policies evolve.

Review this document whenever:

- AI provider terms change.
- Licensing requirements change.
- New asset providers are adopted.
- Platform policies are updated.
- Legal guidance changes.
