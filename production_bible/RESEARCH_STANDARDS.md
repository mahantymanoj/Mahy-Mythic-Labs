# Book I — Research Philosophy

---

# Chapter 1 — Purpose of Research

Research exists to:

* Understand the topic deeply.
* Identify important information.
* Discover meaningful stories.
* Avoid misinformation.
* Build audience trust.

---

# Chapter 2 — Research Principles

Mahy Mythic Labs follows five principles.

---

# 1. Accuracy First

Information must be verified before presentation.

---

# 2. Context Matters

Facts should not be presented without understanding:

* Time period.
* Culture.
* Environment.
* Historical background.

---

# 3. Separate Fact From Interpretation

Every topic may contain:

* Proven facts.
* Academic theories.
* Cultural beliefs.
* Speculation.

These must be clearly separated.

---

# 4. Respect Knowledge Traditions

Mythology, religion, and cultural stories should be presented respectfully.

Avoid:

* Mockery.
* Misrepresentation.
* Unnecessary sensationalism.

---

# 5. Curiosity Over Certainty

Unknown topics should create exploration.

Not false certainty.

---

# Book II — Research Classification System

---

# Chapter 3 — Information Categories

Every research note should classify information.

---

## Category A — Established Facts

Information supported by strong evidence.

Examples:

* Historical dates.
* Scientific measurements.
* Documented events.

Confidence:

High.

---

## Category B — Academic Theories

Ideas supported by research but still debated.

Examples:

* Historical interpretations.
* Scientific hypotheses.

Confidence:

Medium.

---

## Category C — Cultural Beliefs

Stories, traditions, and interpretations.

Examples:

* Mythological narratives.
* Folklore.

Confidence:

Context-dependent.

---

## Category D — Speculation

Possibilities without strong evidence.

Examples:

* Future predictions.
* Unknown mysteries.

Confidence:

Low.

---

# Chapter 4 — Research Labeling

Every important statement should be marked:

Example:

```text
FACT:

The Roman Empire existed from 27 BCE.

SOURCE:

Historical reference.


THEORY:

A possible explanation for...


BELIEF:

According to ancient tradition...


SPECULATION:

Some researchers suggest...
```

# Book V — Topic Specific Research Standards

---

# Chapter 12 — Mythology Research

When covering mythology:

Include:

* Origin.
* Cultural background.
* Symbolism.
* Different interpretations.

Avoid presenting:

* Beliefs as proven scientific facts.
* One interpretation as the only truth.

---

# Chapter 13 — History Research

Include:

* Timeline.
* Historical context.
* Primary sources when available.
* Different perspectives.

Avoid:

* Removing important context.
* Oversimplifying complex events.

---

# Chapter 14 — Science Research

Include:

* Scientific explanation.
* Current understanding.
* Evidence.
* Limitations.

Avoid:

* Presenting theories as facts.
* Exaggerating discoveries.

---

# Chapter 15 — Future Technology Research

Include:

* Current capabilities.
* Research direction.
* Possibilities.
* Limitations.

Avoid:

* Unrealistic predictions.
