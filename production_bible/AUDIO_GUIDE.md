# Preface

Visuals create the world.

Audio creates the emotion.

A viewer may watch a beautiful scene, but sound determines how they experience that scene.

A temple without sound feels empty.

A galaxy without atmosphere feels distant.

A historical moment without emotional music feels ordinary.

Mahy Mythic Labs treats audio as a storytelling layer, not a technical requirement.

The goal:

> Create an immersive audio experience that guides emotion, understanding, and curiosity.

# Chapter 8 — Narration Recording Standards

---

## Speed

* Too slow.

# Book IV — Music Direction

---

# Chapter 11 — Music Philosophy

Music should support:

* Emotion.
* Atmosphere.
* Story progression.

Music should never compete with narration.

---

# Chapter 12 — Music Categories

---

# Ancient / Mythology Music

Characteristics:

* Traditional instruments.
* Spiritual atmosphere.
* Organic sounds.

Possible elements:

* Flute.
* Strings.
* Bells.
* Choir.
* Percussion.

---

# Documentary Music

Characteristics:

* Emotional orchestra.
* Soft background textures.
* Progressive development.

---

# Science Music

Characteristics:

* Ambient.
* Futuristic.
* Minimal.

Possible elements:

* Synth textures.
* Electronic atmosphere.

---

# Space Music

Characteristics:

* Vast.
* Slow.
* Expansive.

Possible elements:

* Deep drones.
* Atmospheric pads.
* Cosmic textures.

---

# Epic Music

Used for:

* Major discoveries.
* Climaxes.
* Final revelations.

Characteristics:

* Orchestra.
* Choir.
* Powerful rhythm.

# Book VI — Audio Mixing Standards

---

# Chapter 16 — Audio Balance

The listener should always understand:

1. Narration.

2. Important sound effects.

3. Music atmosphere.

---

# Chapter 17 — Mixing Philosophy

Narration:

Highest priority.

Music:

Emotional support.

Effects:

Immersion.

---

# Chapter 18 — Audio Transitions

Use:

* Smooth fades.
* Natural transitions.
* Emotional timing.

Avoid:

* Sudden volume changes.
* Harsh cuts.
