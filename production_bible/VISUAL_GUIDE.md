# Preface

Visual storytelling is the foundation of Mahy Mythic Labs.

Before the audience hears the narration, they experience the image.

A single frame should communicate:

* Emotion.
* Scale.
* Story.
* Atmosphere.
* Meaning.

Artificial Intelligence can generate unlimited images.

The responsibility of Mahy Mythic Labs is to create intentional visuals.

The goal is not:

"Generate beautiful images."

The goal is:

"Create visuals that tell unforgettable stories."

The most important element.

Secondary Focus:

Supporting details.

Background:

Creates context.

Show emotion and detail.
