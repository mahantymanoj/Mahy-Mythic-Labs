# Color Palette — Mahy Mythic Labs

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the official color system used across:

* Videos
* Thumbnails
* Logos
* Graphics
* Animations
* Social media

The goal is to create a recognizable visual identity.

---

# 2. Brand Color Philosophy

The Mahy Mythic Labs visual identity combines:

```text
Cosmic Mystery

+

Ancient Wisdom

+

Future Technology
```

---

# 3. Primary Colors

## Cosmic Deep Blue

Purpose:

Represents:

* Universe
* Mystery
* Space
* Depth

Usage:

* Backgrounds
* Space scenes
* Cinematic environments

---

## Ancient Gold

Purpose:

Represents:

* Knowledge
* Wisdom
* Divine elements

Usage:

* Highlights
* Symbols
* Important objects

---

## Mystic Purple

Purpose:

Represents:

* Mystery
* Consciousness
* Unknown realms

Usage:

* Cosmic effects
* Magical atmosphere

---

# 4. Secondary Colors

## Silver

Represents:

* Technology
* Futuristic elements

Usage:

* UI elements
* Scientific visuals

---

## White

Represents:

* Purity
* Light
* Knowledge

Usage:

* Text
* Energy effects

---

## Black

Represents:

* Space
* Unknown
* Mystery

Usage:

* Contrast
* Background depth

---

# 5. Visual Mood

Preferred combinations:

```text
Dark Blue

+

Gold Light

+

Purple Cosmic Glow
```

Creates:

* Premium documentary feeling
* Cosmic atmosphere
* Ancient-future balance

---

# 6. Avoid

Avoid excessive use of:

* Bright neon colors
* Cartoon colors
* Random palettes
* Low contrast combinations

---

# 7. Thumbnail Color Strategy

Primary:

Dark background

*

Bright subject

*

Strong contrast

Example:

```text
Dark Space

+

Golden Character

+

White Highlight
```

---

# 8. Cinematic Grading Style

Preferred:

* High contrast
* Deep shadows
* Soft highlights
* Atmospheric lighting

---

# 9. Brand Rule

Every visual should feel like:

```text
A documentary discovered inside a cosmic archive.
```
