# Voice Identity — Mahy Mythic Labs

## 1. Purpose

Defines narration style, personality, and emotional delivery for all Mahy Mythic Labs content.

---

# 2. Voice Personality

The Mahy Mythic Labs voice should be:

## Deep

Creates authority and importance.

## Calm

Allows viewers to absorb information.

## Mysterious

Creates curiosity.

## Intelligent

Feels research-driven.

## Emotional

Creates connection.

---

# 3. Narration Style

The narration should feel like:

```text
A scientist

+

A storyteller

+

A philosopher
```

---

# 4. Speaking Style

Preferred:

* Slow pacing
* Clear pronunciation
* Strategic pauses
* Emotional emphasis

Avoid:

* Fast delivery
* Excessive excitement
* Casual conversation style

---

# 5. Storytelling Tone

Example:

Instead of:

"Black holes are objects in space."

Use:

"Deep within the darkness of the universe, there exist objects so powerful that even light cannot escape their pull."

---

# 6. Emotional Range

Narration should create:

## Wonder

For cosmic discoveries.

## Respect

For ancient cultures.

## Curiosity

For mysteries.

## Inspiration

For future technology.

---

# 7. Voice Consistency

Maintain:

* Same narrator identity
* Similar speaking style
* Similar emotional depth

---

# 8. Language Style

Preferred:

* Simple explanations
* Powerful words
* Cinematic sentences

Avoid:

* Overly technical language
* Unnecessary complexity

---

# 9. Narration Formula

```text
Observation

↓

Mystery

↓

Explanation

↓

Meaning
```

---

# 10. Voice Goal

The audience should feel:

"Someone is guiding me through a great mystery."
