# Source Verification Framework

Every source should be evaluated using:

## Authority

Who created the information?

Examples:

* Universities
* Research organizations
* Scientific institutions

---

## Accuracy

Is the information:

* Evidence-based?
* Peer-reviewed?
* Supported by multiple sources?

---

## Relevance

Does the source support the specific topic?

---

## Date

Check:

* Publication date
* Latest research updates
* Historical context

---

# Evidence Classification

AI agents should classify information as:

## Confirmed Fact

Strong evidence exists.

Example:

"The Earth orbits the Sun."

---

## Scientific Theory

A well-supported explanation.

Example:

"Evolution by natural selection."

---

## Hypothesis

A possible explanation requiring further evidence.

---

## Speculation

Creative possibility without confirmed evidence.
