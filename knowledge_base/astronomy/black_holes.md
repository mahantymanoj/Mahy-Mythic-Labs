# Black Holes

## Overview

A black hole is a region of spacetime where gravity is extremely strong.

Nothing can escape after crossing the event horizon, including light.

---

# Formation

Stellar black holes form when massive stars collapse after exhausting their nuclear fuel.

---

# Structure

## Singularity

Theoretical center where matter is compressed to extreme density.

---

## Event Horizon

Boundary beyond which escape is impossible.

---

## Accretion Disk

Hot rotating material surrounding a black hole.

---

# Types of Black Holes

## Stellar Black Holes

Created from massive stars.

---

## Intermediate Black Holes

Possible category between stellar and supermassive black holes.

---

## Supermassive Black Holes

Found at galaxy centers.

---

# Famous Examples

## Sagittarius A*

Located at the center of the Milky Way.

---

## M87*

First black hole image captured in 2019.
