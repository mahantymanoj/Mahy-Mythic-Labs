# Planets

## Overview

Planets are celestial bodies orbiting stars.

They may contain:

* Rock
* Gas
* Ice
* Oceans
* Atmospheres

---

# Solar System

Our Solar System contains:

* Sun
* Eight planets
* Moons
* Asteroids
* Comets

---

## Gas Giants

Examples:

---

## Ice Giants

Examples:

* Uranus

# Earth

Unique characteristics:

* Liquid water
* Stable atmosphere
* Life-supporting conditions

# Exoplanets

Planets outside our Solar System.

Scientists search for:

* Habitable zones
* Atmospheric signals
* Signs of life
