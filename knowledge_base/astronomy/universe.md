# The Universe

## Overview

The universe is the complete system containing:

* Space
* Time
* Matter
* Energy
* Physical laws

Current scientific understanding estimates the universe is approximately **13.8 billion years old**.

---

# Origin of the Universe

## Big Bang Theory

The Big Bang theory explains that the universe began from an extremely hot and dense state and has been expanding ever since.

Important points:

* It was not an explosion inside existing space
* Space itself expanded
* Matter and energy evolved over time

---

# Timeline of Cosmic Evolution

## Planck Era

Approximate time:

```
0 - 10⁻⁴³ seconds
```

Physics as currently understood cannot fully describe this period.

---

## Formation of Particles

The early universe cooled, allowing fundamental particles to form.

Created:

* Protons
* Neutrons
* Electrons

---

## Formation of Atoms

After hundreds of thousands of years:

* Hydrogen atoms formed
* Light traveled freely
* Cosmic Microwave Background was created

---

## Formation of Stars

Gravity gathered clouds of gas.

First stars:

* Created heavier elements
* Changed cosmic chemistry

---

# Structure of the Universe

The universe contains:

* Galaxies
* Galaxy clusters
* Superclusters
* Cosmic web

---

# Dark Matter

Dark matter is an unknown form of matter detected through gravitational effects.

Properties:

* Does not emit light
* Influences galaxy movement
* Nature remains unknown

---

# Dark Energy

Dark energy is a theoretical component responsible for accelerated expansion of the universe.
