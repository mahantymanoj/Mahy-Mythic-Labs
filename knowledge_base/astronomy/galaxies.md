# Galaxies

## Overview

A galaxy is a massive collection of:

* Stars
* Gas
* Dust
* Dark matter

held together by gravity.

---

# Galaxy Types

## Spiral Galaxies

Characteristics:

* Rotating disk structure
* Spiral arms
* Active star formation

Example:

Milky Way Galaxy

---

## Elliptical Galaxies

Characteristics:

* Rounded shape
* Older stars
* Less active star formation

---

## Irregular Galaxies

Characteristics:

* No defined structure
* Often shaped by gravitational interactions

---

# Milky Way Galaxy

The Milky Way is our home galaxy.

Characteristics:

* Spiral galaxy
* Contains hundreds of billions of stars
* Contains our Solar System

---

# Galaxy Formation

Galaxies formed through:

* Gravity
* Gas collapse
* Mergers between galaxies

---

# Galactic Collisions

Galaxies can merge over billions of years.

Effects:

* New star formation
* Shape changes
* Black hole interactions

---

# Supermassive Black Holes

Most large galaxies contain a massive black hole at their center.

Example:

Milky Way:

Sagittarius A*
