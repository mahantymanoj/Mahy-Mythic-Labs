# Stars

## Overview

Stars are massive spheres of plasma powered by nuclear fusion.

They produce:

* Light
* Heat
* Heavy elements

---

# Stellar Formation

Stars form inside:

* Molecular clouds
* Nebulae

Gravity compresses gas until fusion begins.

---

# Nuclear Fusion

Stars convert hydrogen into helium.

Energy is released as:

* Light
* Heat

---

# Star Lifecycle

## Main Sequence

Stable hydrogen fusion phase.

---

## Red Giant

Star expands after hydrogen fuel decreases.

---

## Supernova

Massive stars explode.

Creates elements:

* Iron
* Gold
* Other heavy elements

---

# Stellar Remnants

Possible outcomes:

* White dwarf
* Neutron star
* Black hole

---

# Importance of Stars

Elements in human bodies were created inside ancient stars.

Scientific idea:

"We are made of star material."

---

* "The Life and Death of Stars"
