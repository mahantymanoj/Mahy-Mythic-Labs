# Ancient Civilizations

## Overview

Ancient civilizations are complex societies that developed systems of:

* Government
* Writing
* Architecture
* Science
* Trade
* Culture

---

## Region

Modern region:

Iraq and surrounding areas.

---

## Major Contributions

* Early writing systems
* Cities
* Law codes
* Mathematics
* Astronomy

## Important Cultures

* Sumerians
* Akkadians
* Babylonians
* Assyrians
