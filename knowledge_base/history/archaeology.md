# Archaeology

## Overview

Archaeology is the scientific study of past human societies through physical remains.

---

# Archaeological Evidence

Sources include:

## Artifacts

Objects created or modified by humans.

Examples:

* Tools
* Weapons
* Jewelry
* Pottery

---

## Structures

Examples:

* Temples
* Houses
* Fortifications
* Monuments

---

## Human Remains

Used to understand:

* Biology
* Health
* Lifestyle
* Migration

---

# Archaeological Methods

## Excavation

Systematic digging to uncover historical layers.

---

## Dating Techniques

## Radiocarbon Dating

Used for organic materials.

Examples:

* Wood
* Bone
* Plant remains

---

## Stratigraphy

Studies layers of soil and archaeological deposits.
