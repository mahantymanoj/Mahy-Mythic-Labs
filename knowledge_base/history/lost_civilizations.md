# Lost Civilizations

## Overview

Lost civilizations are societies that declined, disappeared, or were transformed over time.

Reasons include:

* Climate change
* War
* Economic collapse
* Migration
* Environmental challenges

---

# Indus Valley Civilization

Mysteries:

* Decline of major cities
* Undeciphered script
* Changing environment

Known facts:

* Advanced urban planning
* Extensive trade networks

---

# Maya Civilization

The Maya civilization developed advanced:

* Mathematics
* Astronomy
* Architecture

Their decline involved multiple factors:

* Environmental stress
* Political changes
* Warfare

---

# Ancient Egypt's Forgotten Cities

Many Egyptian settlements changed due to:

* River movement
* Political changes
* Economic shifts

---

# Atlantis

## Overview

Atlantis is a legendary island civilization described by Plato.

Historical status:

* No confirmed archaeological evidence
* Considered by many scholars as philosophical storytelling

---

# Pompeii

A Roman city preserved after Mount Vesuvius eruption.

Importance:

* Provides insight into Roman daily life
