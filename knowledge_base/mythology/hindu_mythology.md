# Hindu Mythology

## Overview

Hindu mythology represents a vast collection of sacred narratives, philosophical ideas, and cultural traditions originating from the Indian subcontinent.

It contains stories about:

* Creation of the cosmos
* Gods and divine beings
* Human existence
* Cycles of time
* Moral principles

---

# Major Textual Sources

## Vedas

The oldest sacred texts of Hindu tradition.

Includes:

* Hymns
* Ritual knowledge
* Philosophical ideas

---

## Upanishads

Focus on:

* Consciousness
* Reality
* Self-knowledge

Important concept:

Brahman — ultimate reality

Atman — individual self

---

## Puranas

Collections of stories about:

* Gods
* Creation
* Cosmic events
* Dynasties

Major examples:

* Vishnu Purana
* Shiva Purana
* Bhagavata Purana

---

# Major Deities

## Brahma

Associated with:

* Creation
* Cosmic origin

---

## Vishnu

Associated with:

* Preservation
* Protection of cosmic order

Important avatars:

* Rama
* Krishna

---

## Shiva

Associated with:

* Transformation
* Destruction and renewal
* Cosmic consciousness

Forms:

* Nataraja
* Mahadeva

---

## Devi

The divine feminine principle.

Forms include:

* Durga
* Kali
* Lakshmi
* Saraswati

---

# Cosmic Concepts

## Yugas

Time is viewed as repeating cycles.

Four ages:

* Satya Yuga
* Treta Yuga
* Dvapara Yuga
* Kali Yuga

---

## Pralaya

Concept of cosmic dissolution and renewal.

---

## Lokas

Different realms of existence described in traditional cosmology.

---

# Important Stories

## Samudra Manthan

The cosmic ocean churning.

Themes:

* Cooperation
* Divine conflict
* Creation of powerful objects

---

## Bhagavad Gita

Dialogue between:

* Krishna
* Arjuna

Themes:

* Duty
* Ethics
* Spiritual knowledge
