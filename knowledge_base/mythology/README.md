# What is Mythology?

Mythology is a collection of traditional stories, beliefs, symbols, and narratives developed by civilizations to explain:

* Creation of the universe
* Origins of humanity
* Natural phenomena
* Gods and supernatural beings
* Human values and morality
* The relationship between humans and cosmos

# Research Guidelines

When using mythology content:

## Separate:

Historical Facts:

* Archaeological evidence
* Ancient texts
* Cultural practices

Traditional Beliefs:

* Religious narratives
* Oral traditions
* Symbolic interpretations

Modern Interpretations:

* Popular culture
* Fictional adaptations

---

# Storytelling Principles

Mahy Mythic Labs mythology content should:

* Respect cultural traditions
* Avoid mocking beliefs
* Explain context
* Separate mythology from proven science
* Present multiple perspectives
