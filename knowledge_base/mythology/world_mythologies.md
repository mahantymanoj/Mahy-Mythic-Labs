# World Mythologies

## Overview

Human civilizations across the world developed mythology to explain existence, nature, and human experience.

---

# Common Mythological Themes

## Creation Stories

Many cultures have stories explaining:

* Origin of universe
* Birth of gods
* Creation of humanity

---

## Flood Myths

Flood stories appear in many traditions.

Examples:

* Manu story (Indian tradition)
* Noah's Ark (Abrahamic traditions)
* Epic of Gilgamesh

---

## Hero Journey

Common pattern:

1. Ordinary world
2. Challenge
3. Transformation
4. Return
