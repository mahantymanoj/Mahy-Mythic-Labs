# Egyptian Mythology

## Overview

Ancient Egyptian mythology developed around concepts of:

* Creation
* Divine order
* Death and rebirth
* The afterlife

---

# Creation Concepts

Different traditions describe creation through different gods.

Important creator figures:

* Atum
* Ra
* Ptah

---

* Sun

* Kingship
