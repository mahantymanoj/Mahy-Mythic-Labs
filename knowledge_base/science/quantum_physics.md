# Quantum Physics

## Overview

Quantum physics studies nature at extremely small scales:

* Atoms
* Electrons
* Photons
* Subatomic particles

At this scale, reality behaves differently from everyday experience.

---

# Quantum Concepts

## Quantum Superposition

Particles can exist in multiple possible states until measured.

---

## Quantum Entanglement

Particles can share correlated states even when separated.

Einstein described this as:

"spooky action at a distance"

---

## Wave-Particle Duality

Quantum objects can behave as:

* Waves
* Particles

Example:

Light

---

# Quantum Fields

Modern physics describes particles as excitations in quantum fields.

Examples:

* Electromagnetic field
* Higgs field

---

# Uncertainty Principle

Proposed by Werner Heisenberg.

States that certain properties cannot be simultaneously measured with unlimited precision.

Example:

* Position
* Momentum
