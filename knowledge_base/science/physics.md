# Physics

## Overview

Physics is the fundamental science that studies:

* Matter
* Energy
* Motion
* Forces
* Space
* Time
* The laws governing the universe

---

# Fundamental Forces

The universe is governed by four known fundamental forces.

---

## Gravity

Controls:

* Planetary motion
* Stars
* Galaxies
* Large-scale cosmic structures

Described by:

* Newton's laws
* Einstein's General Relativity

---

## Electromagnetism

Responsible for:

* Light
* Electricity
* Magnetism
* Chemical bonding

---

## Strong Nuclear Force

Holds together:

* Atomic nuclei
* Protons
* Neutrons

---

## Weak Nuclear Force

Responsible for:

* Radioactive decay
* Nuclear reactions inside stars

---

# Classical Physics

## Newtonian Mechanics

Explains:

* Motion
* Forces
* Gravity

Three laws:

1. Objects maintain motion unless acted upon
2. Force causes acceleration
3. Every action has an equal reaction

---

# Relativity

## Special Relativity

Introduced:

* Time dilation
* Space-time relationship
* E = mc²

---

## General Relativity

Explains gravity as the curvature of spacetime.

---

# Thermodynamics

Studies:

* Heat
* Energy transfer
* Entropy

Important concept:

Energy cannot be created or destroyed.

---

# Particle Physics

Studies fundamental particles:

Examples:

* Quarks
* Electrons
* Neutrinos

---

# Cosmological Physics

Explains:

* Universe origin
* Black holes
* Dark matter
* Dark energy
