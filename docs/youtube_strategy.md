# YouTube Strategy — Mahy Mythic Labs

The **Mahy Mythic Labs YouTube Strategy** defines the framework for growing the channel through consistent, high-quality, cinematic content.

This document covers:

* YouTube channel positioning
* Audience growth strategy
* Content publishing system
* Search optimization
* Click-through rate improvement
* Viewer retention strategy
* Community building
* Analytics-driven optimization

The goal is to build Mahy Mythic Labs into a recognizable cinematic AI documentary channel.

# 8. Title Strategy

## Objective

Create curiosity while accurately representing the content.

---

## Title Formula

```text
Keyword

+

Mystery

+

Emotional Trigger
```

Example:

Weak:

```text
Shiva Information
```

Strong:

```text
The Cosmic Mystery Hidden Behind Shiva's Third Eye
```

---

## Title Rules

Avoid:

* Fake claims
* Misleading promises
* Excessive clickbait

Use:

* Questions
* Curiosity
* Discovery
* Mystery

---

# 9. Thumbnail Strategy

## Thumbnail Goal

The thumbnail must communicate:

"What is this video about?"

within one second.

---

## Thumbnail Design Rules

### Main Subject

Use one dominant element:

Examples:

* Mythological character
* Planet
* Ancient structure
* Cosmic object

---

### Composition

Structure:

```text
Foreground:
Main Subject

Background:
Environment / Mystery

Text:
Optional
```

---

### Text Rules

Maximum:

```text
3-5 words
```

Examples:

Good:

```text
THE HIDDEN TRUTH
```

Bad:

```text
Everything You Need To Know About Ancient Indian Cosmic Knowledge
```

# 12. Audience Retention Strategy

Retention improves through:

## Story Progression

Every minute should answer one question and create another.

---

## Pattern Interrupts

Every 20-40 seconds introduce:

* New visual
* New information
* New question
* New perspective

---

## Visual Variety

Avoid:

* Static images too long
* Repetitive scenes

Use:

* Camera movement
* Animations
* Transitions
* Environmental changes
