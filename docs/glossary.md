# Glossary

**Project:** Mahy Mythic Labs Studio OS

**Document Version:** 1.0

**Last Updated:** 05-Aug-2026

# Purpose

The Glossary serves as the central reference for terminology used throughout the Mahy Mythic Labs Studio OS.

Its purpose is to ensure consistency in communication, documentation, prompt writing, storytelling, cinematography, AI workflows, and video production.

This document is a living resource and should be updated whenever new terms, techniques, or technologies become part of the production process.

---

# How to Use This Document

* Add new terms alphabetically.
* Keep definitions concise and easy to understand.
* Use examples whenever helpful.
* Avoid duplicate definitions.
* Update this glossary whenever a new production concept becomes part of the workflow.

---

# A

## AI Director

The AI system responsible for coordinating the entire creative workflow, including research, storytelling, prompt generation, scene planning, narration, and production guidance.

---

## Aspect Ratio

The proportional relationship between the width and height of an image or video.

Examples:

* 16:9 – YouTube Landscape
* 9:16 – YouTube Shorts
* 1:1 – Social Media

---

# B

## B-Roll

Supplementary footage used to support the main narration or story.

Example:

While explaining an ancient temple, aerial shots, sculptures, and landscapes are used as B-roll.

---

## Background Score

Instrumental music used to enhance emotion, suspense, atmosphere, or storytelling without distracting from the narration.

---

# C

## Cinematic Shot

A visually appealing camera shot designed to create emotion, realism, or dramatic impact.

Examples:

* Wide Shot
* Close-Up
* Tracking Shot
* Drone Shot
* Low Angle Shot

---

## Color Grading

The process of adjusting colors, contrast, shadows, highlights, and mood to achieve a consistent cinematic look.

---

## Continuity

The process of maintaining consistency between scenes, including characters, costumes, lighting, environments, and story progression.

---

# D

## Dolly Shot

A camera movement where the camera physically moves toward or away from the subject to create depth and immersion.

---

## Documentary Style

A storytelling approach that presents factual information using cinematic visuals, narration, interviews, and supporting evidence.

---

# E

## Establishing Shot

The opening shot of a scene that introduces the location, environment, and atmosphere.

---

## Episode

A complete YouTube production project containing research, scripts, prompts, assets, editing files, and publishing materials.

---

# F

## Fact Validation

The process of verifying information using trusted historical, scientific, or academic sources before including it in a video.

---

## Frame Rate (FPS)

The number of frames displayed per second in a video.

Common values:

* 24 FPS – Cinematic
* 30 FPS – Standard Video
* 60 FPS – Smooth Motion

---

# G

## Generative AI

Artificial Intelligence capable of generating text, images, audio, music, or video based on prompts.

---

# H

## Hook

The opening portion of a video designed to capture the viewer's attention within the first few seconds.

---

# I

## Image Prompt

A structured instruction used by an AI image generation model to create a desired visual.

---

## Image-to-Video

The process of converting a still image into an animated video using AI video generation models.

---

# K

## Keyframe

An important frame that defines the beginning or end of an animation or camera movement.

---

# L

## LUT (Look-Up Table)

A predefined color profile used to achieve a consistent visual style during color grading.

---

# M

## Master Director

The primary AI instruction set that defines how AI should behave while assisting with content creation for Mahy Mythic Labs.

---

## Mythology

Traditional narratives, beliefs, and cultural stories passed down through generations.

Within Mahy Mythic Labs, mythology is presented respectfully while distinguishing tradition, interpretation, and historical evidence where appropriate.

---

# N

## Narration

The spoken storytelling that guides viewers through the video.

Narration style may vary depending on the content:

* Calm & Spiritual
* Documentary
* Epic
* Suspenseful

---

# P

## Production Bible

The collection of standards, workflows, and creative principles that define how Mahy Mythic Labs produces content.

---

## Prompt

A structured instruction provided to an AI model to generate text, images, audio, or video.

---

# R

## Research

The systematic process of collecting, validating, organizing, and documenting information before creating a script.

---

## Rendering

The process of exporting the final edited video into a publishable format.

---

# S

## Scene

A single visual segment of a video representing one location, event, or part of the story.

---

## Script

The written narrative that includes dialogue, narration, scene descriptions, and production notes.

---

## Storyboard

A visual plan illustrating each scene before image or video generation begins.

---

# T

## Thumbnail

The preview image displayed on YouTube that encourages viewers to click on the video.

---

## Timeline

The editing workspace where video clips, audio, music, effects, and transitions are arranged sequentially.

---

# V

## Video Prompt

A structured instruction used by an AI video generation model to create animated scenes.

---

## Voice-over

Recorded narration synchronized with the visuals to explain or tell the story.

---

# W

## Wide Shot

A camera angle showing the complete environment and providing context before moving to closer shots.

---

## Workflow

The standardized sequence of steps followed to produce every Mahy Mythic Labs video.

---

# Y

## YouTube SEO

The practice of optimizing titles, descriptions, keywords, tags, thumbnails, and metadata to improve discoverability on YouTube.
