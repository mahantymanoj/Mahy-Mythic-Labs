# Mahy Mythic Labs — Brand Assets

## Brand Name

**Mahy Mythic Labs**

## Positioning

Cinematic AI Storytelling Laboratory.

Mahy Mythic Labs creates premium documentary stories at the intersection of mythology, ancient civilizations, science, astronomy, human evolution, philosophy, and the future of AI.

## Brand Promise

Every asset should communicate:

- Wonder without misinformation
- Cinematic scale with intellectual clarity
- Ancient memory connected to cosmic possibility
- Premium, atmospheric, documentary-quality storytelling

## Core Asset Set

| Asset | Purpose | Required variants |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary logo | Main channel identity | Light, dark, transparent |
| Wordmark | Headers, end cards, social graphics | Light, dark |
| Icon / mark | Profile image, watermark, favicon | Light, dark, monochrome |
| YouTube banner | Channel identity and promise | Desktop and mobile-safe versions |
| End card | Episode close | 16:9 and 9:16 |
| Lower third | Names, locations, dates, key facts | Standard and compact |
| Title cards | Episode and chapter openings | 16:9 and 9:16 |
| Thumbnail system | Video packaging | Long-form and Shorts variants |

## Asset Rules

- Use only approved logos and brand marks.
- Preserve logo proportions; never stretch, rotate, outline, or add effects that reduce legibility.
- Keep master editable files separate from exported production files.
- Use transparent PNG or SVG for logos and overlays whenever possible.
- Version files using lowercase kebab case:

```text
brand-[asset-name]-[variant]-v##.[extension]
```

Example:

```text
brand-wordmark-light-v01.svg
```

## Brand Keywords

- Cinematic — every visual must feel like a documentary frame.
- Mythic — rooted in the ancient, symbolic, and legendary.
- Intelligent — grounded in research and presented with clarity.
- Cosmic — open to the universe in scale and imagination.
- Ancient — honoring human history, culture, and memory.
- Precise — nothing extraneous; every element earns its place.
- Atmospheric — mood and light carry as much meaning as subject.
- Human — the story always returns to people and their place in the world.

## Quality Standard

A brand asset is approved only when it feels:

- Premium
- Legible at small size
- Appropriate for a global audience
- Consistent with documentary storytelling
- Free from generic AI-fantasy visual clichés
