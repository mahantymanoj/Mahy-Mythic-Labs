# Music Assets

## Purpose

This folder stores original, licensed, and approved music used in Mahy Mythic Labs productions.

## Allowed Content

- Original music tracks
- Licensed music tracks
- Music stems
- Loops
- Alternate edits
- Cue sheets
- License records and attribution notes

## File Naming

Use:

`[mood-or-theme]-[track-name]-[variant]-v##.[extension]`

Examples:

`cosmic-discovery-rising-tension-v01.wav`

`ancient-mystery-low-drone-v02.wav`

`ep001-antikythera-music-edit-v01.wav`

## Required Metadata

Each music asset must record:

- Asset ID
- Track title
- Composer or source
- License type
- Commercial-use status
- Attribution requirement
- Duration
- Mood and pacing
- Allowed usage scope
- License expiry date, if applicable

## Music Direction

Mahy Mythic Labs music should feel:

- Cinematic
- Atmospheric
- Intelligent
- Emotionally restrained
- Supportive of narration
- Appropriate to the historical, scientific, or cosmic subject

Avoid overly generic trailer music, excessive drops, distracting percussion, or music that overwhelms the narration.

## Rules

- Use only original or commercially licensed music.
- Store proof of license in `assets/licenses/`.
- Do not use unverified music from social platforms or search results.
- Record music use and timestamps in the episode cue sheet.
- Never overwrite an approved track; create a new version.
