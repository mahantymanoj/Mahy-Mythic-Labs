## File Naming

Use:

`music-[category]-[track-name]-[variant]-v##.[extension]`

Examples:

```text
music-cosmic-first-light-main-v01.wav
music-ancient-bronze-mystery-loop-v01.wav
music-emotional-civilization-finale-v02.wav

## Required Metadata

Every music asset must record:

- Music ID
- Track name
- Composer or source
- License ID
- Commercial-use status
- Attribution requirements
- Modification permission
- Duration
- BPM, if relevant
- Key, if known
- Mood and intended narrative use
- Approval status

## Music Direction

Music should feel:

- Cinematic
- Atmospheric
- Intelligent
- Emotionally controlled
- Supportive of narration
- Appropriate to the story’s culture, era, and scale

Avoid generic trailer music, excessive dramatic drops, distracting percussion, or music that overpowers the narration.

## Usage Rules

- Use only original or commercially licensed music.
- Record every third-party track in `assets/licenses/asset_license_tracker.md`.
- Do not use copyrighted music from films, television, games, streaming platforms, or social media without explicit permission.
- Keep original source files, license documents, stems, and cue information.
- Do not overwrite approved tracks; create a new version for edits.
- Record episode use and timestamps in the relevant episode cue sheet.
- Keep reusable library music separate from episode-specific edits.

## Approval Checklist

- [ ] Source and license are verified.
- [ ] Commercial use is permitted.
- [ ] Attribution requirements are documented.
- [ ] Music is appropriate for the intended story and culture.
- [ ] Audio quality is clean and production-ready.
